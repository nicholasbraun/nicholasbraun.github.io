# Services

Every engagement starts with a short scoping call. Afterwards you get a written
scope and a fixed price, per project or per month — no open-ended hourly
billing.

---

## New backend service — from zero to production

A Go or Node.js service designed, built and running in your infrastructure: API
spec, data model, tests, CI pipeline, deployment and monitoring. Handed over
with documentation so your team can own it.

## Scale-out readiness

Your service works on one instance but breaks on several. I move in-process
state to Redis, put background work on a queue and make the service safe to run
horizontally — so you add instances when traffic grows instead of
firefighting.

## Framework migration

Moving a backend to a new framework or structure, for example from a
hand-rolled setup to NestJS — module by module, while your team keeps shipping
features.

## Monolith to microservices, step by step

Your monolith slows every team down, but a big-bang rewrite is too risky. I map
the domain boundaries, decide with you which parts are worth extracting — and
which are better served by a well-structured modular monolith — and extract the
first service while the existing system keeps running. Fixed price for the
first extraction; further services as follow-up packages or under monthly
ownership.

## Backend ownership (monthly)

I own a backend or subsystem as part of your team: design, implementation,
tests, deployment and monitoring. Fixed monthly fee for an agreed capacity,
cancellable monthly.
